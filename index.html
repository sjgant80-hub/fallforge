The five phases

What FallForge actually does.

1

Discover

Fetches your existing website and up to 5 signal pages (about · prices · reviews · contact · FAQ). Extracts text · links · social handles · listing URLs (Airbnb, Booking, Google). Detects your vertical with keyword fingerprinting.

2

Kernel distil

Runs the raw evidence through your chosen frontier LLM (default: Claude Opus 4.8). Extracts every fact into the vertical's kernel schema. Populates the not-present list to prevent hallucination. Learns your voice tone from real reply patterns.

3

Wire

Packages your bespoke FallHub install: kernel.json + BOM + install guide + module list. Downloads as a ZIP or (if you provide a GitHub token) pushes directly to a fresh repo. Configures the LLM router with your chosen blend baked in.

4

Calibrate

Post-install, your instance runs a training simulator (WebLLM Botler · free · local). Watches how you reply vs the AI's drafts. Auto-tunes confidence thresholds so your autopilot starts conservative and matches your voice.

5

Handoff

Live URL delivered. What's installed. Why. Cost projection at your chosen blend. Optional teach-me-the-router walkthrough. You slide the autonomy dial as trust grows.

Frequently asked

What you're wondering.

Does my data ever hit your servers?

Local mode: no. Everything runs in your browser. Your website scrape stays local. Your API key stays local. Hosted mode: yes — we run a Cloudflare Worker that helps with heavier fetching and GitHub push. You choose which mode. Sovereign is the default.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. You can run the entire install on WebLLM (free · in-browser Llama 3.1 8B). It's slower but costs nothing forever. If you want faster or higher-quality distillation, plug in an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq, or Mistral. Your key. Direct connection.

What happens after FallForge finishes?

You have a working FallHub instance. Landing page. Autopilot. Training simulator. Modules installed. Router configured. You open the training sim, practice with fake customers, then slide the autonomy dial as you trust it.

What if the vertical detection is wrong?

You can override at install time. The auto-detection is a keyword-fingerprint scan across your site text. If confidence is low, we ask before proceeding. If you're a bespoke niche not on the list, pick "closest fit" and edit the kernel afterwards.

What does the ZIP contain?

kernel.json (your distilled business facts) · bom.json (bill of materials) · README.md · INSTALL.md (step-by-step) · modules-to-install.txt. Drop the kernel into a FallHub fork, install the listed modules, you're live.
