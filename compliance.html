What FallForge does with your data, and what it doesn’t claim.

⚠️ This page is a working draft, not legal advice and not a compliance certification. Every assertion on it must be reviewed and signed off by a qualified UK/EU data-protection + AI-Act adviser before it is relied on. Items marked [VERIFY] depend on how the business is actually run.
0 · The honest posture

Why the architecture starts strong

FallForge’s free tier runs client-side and offline: the task, the examples, and the minted model stay in your browser and on your own machine, via Ollama. AI-Native Solutions does not receive, store, or transmit that data. That makes the product’s compute privacy-by-design (UK GDPR Art 25) and data-minimising (Art 5) by construction. The compliance work is mostly about the business around the tool — the website, enquiries, the done-for-you tier — not the tool’s core.

2 · AI Transparency Notice

FallForge is a tool for creating and evaluating small, task-specific language models. Please note:

  • Outputs are AI-generated and probabilistic — they can be wrong. Keep a human in the loop for anything that matters.
  • The signed scorecard receipt attests measured performance on your own held-out examples at a point in time. It is evidence the model beat a baseline on that sample — not a guarantee of correctness, truth, or fitness for any particular purpose.
  • The model is yours. Once minted it runs on your machine under your control; you are responsible for how you use it.
  • Sample sizes are shown honestly, and a receipt can and will read LOSES or TIES.
3 · Acceptable Use & Scope Limits (EU AI Act–aware)

By using FallForge you agree not to use it to build or operate:

  • Any prohibited AI practice under the EU AI Act (Art 5) — e.g. social scoring, manipulative or exploitative systems, untargeted facial-image scraping, real-time remote biometric ID in public spaces.
  • Any high-risk system (EU AI Act Annex III — biometrics, critical infrastructure, education/vocational selection, employment/recruitment, essential private & public services including creditworthiness, law enforcement, migration, administration of justice) without conducting your own conformity assessment and meeting your obligations as the deployer/provider.
  • Anything unlawful, infringing, or harmful.

You are the controller/deployer for any personal data you process, and for any AI system you build with FallForge. FallForge is a general-purpose tool; the risk classification and the legal obligations attach to your use, not to the tool as delivered.

4 · EU AI Act positioning (internal note → counsel)

What FallForge is: a tool to author and evaluate narrow, few-shot, task-specific models. As delivered it is best read as minimal-risk, and it is not a general-purpose AI model (GPAI) provider — users are not training foundation models. [VERIFY with counsel]

Where obligations land: on the deployer when a user puts a model into a high-risk context. FallForge helps them meet those obligations rather than creating them — the signed receipts + Veridia audit trail (see below) map onto the Act’s record-keeping/logging (Art 12) and technical-documentation/traceability expectations, and support human oversight. This is a feature to lean on in the market, positioned honestly, never as a compliance guarantee.

The accountability artifact, live

Every FallForge mint, shelf listing, and hub receipt is content-hashed and re-verifiable offline — that is the record. The provenance organ walks the real chain from a minted manifest through the shelf to a served node or a Veridia audit and names exactly where it breaks, if it does. That chain is the traceability artifact this section points to — not a separate logging system bolted on afterward.
