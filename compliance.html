◈ AI-Native Solutions · trust & compliance

Private by construction. Built toward GDPR and EU AI Act minimal-risk.

FallForge runs on your machine, offline. Your task, your examples and your model never leave your device — so there is nothing to leak, nothing to breach, and nothing for us to process. Privacy and accountability are not policies bolted on afterward. They are the architecture.

UK GDPR · built by design

Your data never reaches us.

The tool processes your task, your examples and the minted model entirely in your own browser and on your own hardware. AI-Native Solutions does not receive, store or transmit any of it. That makes data minimisation (Art 5) and privacy by design (Art 25) structural, not aspirational — there is no dataset to breach because there is no dataset.

You keep every right under UK GDPR — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection — exercised any time at sjgant80@gmail.com. We set no tracking or advertising cookies. The only personal data we ever hold is what you choose to send us directly, handled under legitimate interest and kept no longer than needed.

EU AI Act · built for the minimal-risk profile

Narrow by design, with the accountability higher-risk systems are asked for.

FallForge builds narrow, task-specific models — the minimal-risk profile under the EU AI Act, which carries no mandatory conformity assessment, no registration and no imposed controls. It is not a general-purpose (GPAI) model provider; you are not training a foundation model.

Even so, every mint, shelf listing and hub receipt is content-hashed and re-verifiable offline — the provenance chain walks it end to end and names any break, live, in your browser. That's the same kind of record-keeping and traceability (Art 12) higher-risk systems are required to keep — built in from day one rather than retrofitted if a use case ever moves up a tier.

AI transparency

Straight about what it is: FallForge creates and evaluates small, task-specific AI models. Outputs are AI-generated — keep a human in the loop for anything that matters. The signed scorecard receipt is evidence your model beat a baseline on your own held-out data — proof of measured performance, not a promise of truth. Once minted, the model is yours, on your machine, under your control.

The one line that protects you

Minimal-risk as delivered. Your deployment sets the rest.

FallForge is a general-purpose tool, so the Act’s risk tier attaches to your use, not to the tool. Put a model into a high-risk job — hiring, credit, biometrics, essential services, law enforcement — and the obligations attach to you as the deployer: run your own conformity assessment first. Prohibited practices (Art 5 — social scoring, manipulation, untargeted biometric scraping) are off-limits, full stop. Everything else — the repetitive, high-volume, unregulated work you’re overpaying to rent — is exactly what this is for.
